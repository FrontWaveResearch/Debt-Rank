Front Wave Research LLP · SEBI Reg. INH000018407

A research instrument. Effective cost of debt is implied: reported FY finance cost divided by average reported total debt, from exchange filings via market data providers (±0.5–1.0pp tolerance). Filings with structurally distorted rates — restructured or subsidised debt, lease-heavy denominators, charges-inflated finance costs, banks — are flagged and excluded from benchmarks but remain searchable with a caution. The review, fair-rate estimates and savings figures are descriptive arithmetic on your inputs and current data: not a credit opinion, an offer, a commitment to finance, or investment advice. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is stored or transmitted.
